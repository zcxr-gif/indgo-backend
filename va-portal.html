Takeoff & landing notifications

Get a Discord post in your own server every time one of your pilots takes off or lands.

How it works

Create a webhook in the Discord channel you want flights posted to (Channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks), paste its URL below and request access. Our team reviews it and switches delivery on for you.

Card appearance

Make the flight card your own — colour, layout and which details it shows. The Inflight logo always stays on the card.

Leave on Default to keep green takeoffs / gold landings.

Compact skips the rendered image for a lighter post.

Large posts the card as a full-width standalone image, not boxed in the embed.

Route (departure → arrival) is always shown. ETE only appears on departures.
